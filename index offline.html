Caregiver guide

Simple reminders for family caregivers. Follow the nurse's instructions. This is not a diagnosis.

Every day

  • Look at the skin, especially heels, hips, lower back and any wound. Note any change.
  • Keep the skin and bedding clean and dry; change position as the nurse has advised.
  • Encourage food and fluids as advised. Wash hands before and after touching a wound.
  • Keep the dressing as the nurse showed you. Do not apply home remedies unless the nurse agrees.

Contact the nurse or go to the hospital now if there is

  • Fever, chills, or the person seems very unwell
  • Redness spreading around the wound
  • Pus or a bad smell
  • Black or dead-looking skin
  • Swelling, warmth or pain getting worse
  • The wound is getting bigger
